These interactive visualisations support the study of microeconomic theory through graphical exploration. Change parameters, compare curves and examine the implications of different constraints and preferences.

**[Browse all nine interactive graphs](/files/interactive_micro_graphs/)**

Each visualisation opens in your browser. No software installation or GitHub account is required.

## Budget constraints and labour–leisure choice

- **[Interactive Budget Function Grapher](/files/interactive_micro_graphs/graphs/budget-function-grapher.html)** — Enter a budget constraint and explore its feasible set, axis intercepts and selected bundles.
- **[Labour–Leisure Budget Constraints](/files/interactive_micro_graphs/graphs/labour-leisure-budget-constraints.html)** — Compare budget lines and feasible labour–leisure bundles.
- **[Labour–Leisure Budget Constraints (24 hours)](/files/interactive_micro_graphs/graphs/labour-leisure-budget-constraints-24hr.html)** — Examine consumption–leisure choices across a 24-hour time endowment.
- **[Labour–Leisure with Indifference Curves (24 hours)](/files/interactive_micro_graphs/graphs/labour-leisure-budget-constraints-24hr-indifference.html)** — Combine budget constraints and indifference curves to analyse labour–leisure choice.

## Preferences, indifference curves and consumer choice

- **[Construct Indifference Curves](/files/interactive_micro_graphs/graphs/construct-indifference-curves.html)** — Explore indifference curves and identify bundles on the same utility level.
- **[Indifference Curves and Budget Constraints](/files/interactive_micro_graphs/graphs/indifference-budget-graph.html)** — Adjust budget constraints and examine consumer choice.
- **[Income and Substitution Effects](/files/interactive_micro_graphs/graphs/income-substitution-effect.html)** — Visualise budget changes and compensation in consumer choice analysis.

## Utility functions and graphical optimisation

- **[Utility and Marginal Utility](/files/interactive_micro_graphs/graphs/utility-marginal-utility.html)** — Explore the utility function and the interpretation of marginal utility.
- **[3D Utility Surface and Budget Constraint](/files/interactive_micro_graphs/graphs/utility-constraint-indifference-3d.html)** — Rotate a utility surface and explore the optimisation problem in three dimensions.

---

*These resources are intended to complement lectures, seminars and independent study. Graphical results should be interpreted in conjunction with the assumptions of the model under examination.*
