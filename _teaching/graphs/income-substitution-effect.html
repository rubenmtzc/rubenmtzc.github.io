Indifference Curves and Budget Constraints

Build the diagram step by step. Add or remove budget lines and up to two indifference curves, then use the compensated line and bundles A, B and C to illustrate substitution, income and total effects.

The graph uses the standard convex Cobb–Douglas preferences U = √(x₁x₂). Each editable budget line has its own p₁, p₂ and income m. Its slope is −p₁/p₂.
