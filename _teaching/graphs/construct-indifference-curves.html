Three indifference curves and selectable bundles

This graph shows three indifference curves only. The middle curve, IC₂, is the reference indifference curve. Use the buttons to move through its bundles one by one, or click directly on any blue reference node. You can also drag the visible text labels with the mouse to reposition them. When you hover over a node, the graph shows its coordinates and the ratio of leisure to consumption. IC₁ and IC₃ each contain one additional node only: one below and one above the reference curve.
