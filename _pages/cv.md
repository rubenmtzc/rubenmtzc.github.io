Research

Research projects

Co-construction of Evidence-Based Resources for Diversifying and Decolonising the Economics Curriculum

Project type: Research project

Funder: Student Success Projects Fund, University of York

Description: This project aims to advance the diversification and decolonisation of the economics curriculum by assessing students’ and staff’s understanding and attitudes, using this insight to create inclusive teaching materials, and collaboratively developing a roadmap for institutional adoption of diversification and decolonisation strategies.

Dates: 27 March 2025 – 14 November 2025

Role: Principal Investigator

Budget: £3,870

Status: Completed

Data dissemination format and willingness to share information

Project type: Research project

Funder: Higher Education Innovation Fund

Description: We explore users’ experience with data access and display formats, and investigate willingness to share information conditional on how information is presented.

Dates: 1 January 2023 – 30 March 2023

Role: Co-investigator

Budget: £3,708

Status: Completed

Engaging the community in designing a smart city

Project type: Research project

Funder: The Alan Turing Institute

Description: This project engaged people from across Leicester to inform policy, shape the delivery of the smart city, and explore how people can benefit from the opportunities that a smart city may provide.

Dates: 20 June 2022 – 30 March 2023

Role: Co-investigator

Budget: £10,000

Status: Completed

Measuring the impact of MedicSpot GP consultations

Project type: Consultancy project

Funder: Willows Health

Description: Evaluation of patient experience and satisfaction, and the cost effectiveness of medical consultations using telemedicine technology.

Dates: 5 August 2021 – 17 December 2021

Role: Principal Investigator
